Privacy policy

Driftwood Isle · last updated 13 October 2026

The short version

Driftwood Isle does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The game makes no network requests while you play.

What stays on your device

Your island (resources, skills, settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to copy your save code yourself from Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

On Android the app only asks for vibration, used for gentle haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Because we collect nothing, the game is safe for players of all ages.

Third parties

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect data under their own policies when you download the app. The game itself contains no third-party SDKs.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address. Questions: open an issue at github.com/gerrit-van-vliet/idle-game.
