Driftwood Isle support

A cozy island idle game · play in your browser · privacy policy

Something not working?

Open an issue at github.com/gerrit-van-vliet/idle-game/issues and tell us your phone model and what happened.

Moving your island to another device

Open Settings → Copy code on the old device, send the code to yourself, then on the new device open Settings → Paste code and tap again to confirm.

Starting over

Settings → Reset game…, then tap again to confirm. This can't be undone.
